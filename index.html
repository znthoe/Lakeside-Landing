Our Story

A Lakeside Institution

Lakeside Landing Kitchen + Bar sits right on the water at Lakeside Inn on South Shore Drive. We're Clear Lake's go-to spot for casual, waterfront dining — whether you're pulling up by boat or walking over from the beach.

From our famous Crab Nachos to the Fish N Chips and Wing & Whiskey Wednesdays, we serve up honest, satisfying food with front-row views of the lake. Cold drinks, warm hospitality, and a fireplace for the cooler evenings.
